# LEXICON — the book's working vocabulary
Rule: every term gets a precise definition, its origin, and its epistemic
passport. Coinages are adopted only after surviving the vise.

**Apparent non-determinism (AND / δ_A)** — behavior not predictable from
an observer's information about circumstances; measured, never asserted
metaphysically. [Aaron; the book's subject]

**The two rulers** — independence (δ_A) and aim; freedom = both at once.
[Ch 4]

**The sealed verdict** — a judgment on your nature delivered from a court
you cannot enter; revoked when the borrowed proof fails to lend.
[Ch 1; replaced Schrödinger's box, Aaron's ruling]

**The gate** — zero-then-jump activation of education/deliberation at
e + s > 1. [T5/T16]

**Stochasticity transfer** — moving variance from a party who would have
to account for it onto parties who cannot. [Aaron via round-2 edits, Ch 7]

**The ledger of imposition** — cascading downward transfer of unabsorbed
variance through a bounded hierarchy; narrows upward, multiplies
downward. [Aaron, Gemini debate; T17 modeling candidate]

**Premium rights** — the two-tier structure of formally universal
rights: a nominal tier (universal) and a responsiveness tier priced in
agency-currency (time past the gate, gate distance, protected privacy).
Exercise costs are denominated in exactly the resources the ledger
tracks, so rights-effectiveness inherits the ledger's distribution.
Gilens & Page 2014 read as field evidence CONSISTENT WITH the
responsiveness tier being priced — never overclaimed as proof.
[Aaron, 2026-08-15; Part V / Applicability candidate]

**Glass sea** — an economy where crowding costs nothing (feeds,
infinitely shareable goods); herding stays individually rational
forever, disabling endogenous decorrelation. [S1 / Ch 11 Lesson Six]

**The broom and the web** — the two wirings' emergent shapes: habit
ruling the trunk vs flips ruling the trunk. [Ch 9, figure v3]

**Cheap theft** — destroying aim while leaving unpredictability intact;
invisible to every single-ruler measure. [Cor 7.1]

**Constants need passports** — no model constant asserted of the world
before a field audit earns it there. [standing guardrail]

**Instrument, not weapon** — the book recruits neutral parties; anger is
never load-bearing. [standing guardrail]

**Mistake budget** — the amount of error an agent's situation lets them
survive; a stealable resource (T18); its premium tier is the purchased
right to err (bailouts). [Aaron, 2026-08-15]

**Frontier leapfrog** — reaching a frontier expands the capacity that
defines the next one; growth as frontier recursion; theft compounds by
foreclosing the victim's contributions. [Aaron, 2026-08-15]

**Role-δ_A** — the instrument applied to positions, top and bottom
symmetrically: what fraction of a role's outcomes is attributable to
occupant vs environment. Field anchor: pay-for-luck literature.
[Aaron, 2026-08-15; anti-binomial guardrail attached]

**Frontier-positive economy** — the ledger run in reverse: T13's
exponential as the return curve on investing in others' independence.
Framed as arithmetic, never advocacy. [Aaron, 2026-08-15]

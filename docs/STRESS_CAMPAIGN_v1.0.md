# Stress campaign against the economic mechanisms (opened 2026-11-09)

Purpose: test every circumstance a critic could raise against the book's economic mechanisms before the
critic does, and print the boundary of each result rather than its best case. Each objective names the
attack it answers, where it runs, and its status. Findings go to THEOREMS_v1.md / TASKS.md and, where
text changes, into the book at defaults.

## Rules of the campaign
Model minus control, each at its own GE; preregistered signs where a sign is claimed; a dated addendum
for every modeling choice made after seeing results; magnitudes are the model's constants, never the
world's; the field audit remains the test.

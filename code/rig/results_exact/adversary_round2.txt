     of the grid: the accuracy-optimal rule for a starved population is that NOBODY acts on their own judgment.
     The reviewer's correction is right, and it proves more than it wants: per-decision accuracy is maximized by
     welding every gate -- which is the shared-tutor town of Lesson 2, N_eff = 1, that starves. Accuracy is not
     survival (T13: nine 0.70 heads beat one 0.80 head). The gate kappa* protects per-decision accuracy; the
     town's famine metric is what independent action protects, and the two objectives part company exactly
     where the reviewer's margin is largest.

A4  ledger of imposition: broadcast vs partition
    broadcast  faced/person by level [100.  40.  20.  14.]  worker share of agency loss = 93.4%
    partition  faced/person by level [100.  10.   0.   0.]  worker share of agency loss = 0.0%
    Entropy accounting (what Ch 13 prices as schedule entropy): a shortfall of k of N shifts, assigned at random:
      N= 20 k= 2: magnitude borne = 2 shift-units (partitioned); uncertainty faced = 20 x h(2/20) = 9.4 bits (broadcast to all 20)
      N= 20 k= 5: magnitude borne = 5 shift-units (partitioned); uncertainty faced = 20 x h(5/20) = 16.2 bits (broadcast to all 20)
      N= 64 k= 8: magnitude borne = 8 shift-units (partitioned); uncertainty faced = 64 x h(8/64) = 34.8 bits (broadcast to all 64)
    Reading: the 93.4% is the broadcast regime. Under pure partition at the printed capacities the hierarchy absorbs
    everything before the worker level (the regional's 20 units of capacity swallow a 10-unit share). The book's
    ledger prices UNCERTAINTY (schedule entropy), which does broadcast -- every worker faces 'will I be cut?' --
    while the MAGNITUDE (lost hours) partitions. T17 must say which it counts; as printed it says 'variance'.

A5  mistake-budget gate with noisy self-knowledge: the accuracy-maximizing kappa*
        s  gate 1-s sigma=0.00 sigma=0.05 sigma=0.10 sigma=0.20 sigma=0.30
     0.30      0.70       1.00        0.98        0.98        1.02        1.41  
     0.50      0.50       1.00        1.02        1.00        0.98        1.00  
     0.70      0.30       1.00        1.02        1.05        0.98        0.72  
    Reading: kappa* = 1 exactly when the gate sits at the population's mean self-knowledge (s = 0.5); above 1 in
    CLEAR worlds (s = 0.3: the gate is high, noisy estimates regress to the mean, a margin protects accuracy);
    BELOW 1 in noisy worlds (s = 0.7). The 'Bayesian necessity of kappa > 1' is a statement about clear worlds
    with a uniform population -- and the sign flips where the book's time-poor live. Externalized losses are a
    separate ledger (who pays), not a correction to the gate.

## Reading

Descriptive only. A cross-section cannot separate what a schedule does to a household from who ends up in such jobs; household income is a band, not a wage; some respondents appear in both years, which the intervals ignore. What the table can show is whether the fragility gap is specific to schedules the employer controls (employer − own) or common to any varying schedule.
